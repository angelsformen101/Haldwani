RULE 1

The In-Person Settlement Imperative

Every legitimate independent companion operates on direct in-person payment. Never transfer money via UPI, PhonePe, Paytm, or net banking prior to seeing the escort in person.

Advance Payment = 100% Scam

Common scam excuses include: "Pay advance for cab driver", "Manager requires security code", "Medical test report clearance fee", or "Pre-booking QR code". All of these are fraudulent schemes operated by cybercriminals. Do not pay a single rupee in advance.

RULE 2

Safety Protocol for Clients

Follow these essential precautions when hiring an independent companion:

1. Choose Reputed Hotels

Book meetings in well-known 3-star or 4-star hotels with proper front desk reception, operational elevators, and security cameras in corridors.

2. Identity Verification

Request a short video greeting or voice note on WhatsApp before confirming the meeting to ensure the person matches their listing photos.

3. Respect Boundaries

Independent companions are professionals who set their own comfort levels. Mutual respect and courteous conduct ensure a safe, pleasant experience.

4. Keep Valuables Secure

Store cash, luxury watches, and credit cards inside hotel room safe boxes as a standard travel security measure.

RULE 3

Safety Guidelines for Independent Companions

If you are an independent model or escort advertising on CityHaldwani, protect your personal well-being:

  • Screen New Callers: Always speak with the client directly on voice call. Trust your instincts—if a caller sounds aggressive, intoxicated, or evasive, decline the booking immediately.
  • Buddy Check-In: Inform a trusted friend or colleague of your general appointment location and agree on a check-in time after your arrival.
  • Verify Hotel Room: Only accept outcalls to recognized hotels with registered guest bookings. Never go to isolated farmhouses, unmarked flats, or parked vehicles.
  • Collect Honorarium First: Ensure all compensation is received respectfully upon arrival before services begin.
RULE 4

Dealing with Extortion, Blackmail & Fake Police Calls

Cybercrime syndicates occasionally run extortion rackets threatening users with "police cases" or "court notices" if money is not transferred to a UPI QR code.

What to do if someone threatens you:
  1. Do Not Panic: Real police departments never send extortion notices over WhatsApp or demand UPI payments.
  2. Do Not Pay: Paying once invites repeated extortion demands. Block the caller on WhatsApp and your phone.
  3. Dial 1930: Call India's national cybercrime financial fraud helpline at 1930 or register a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in.
